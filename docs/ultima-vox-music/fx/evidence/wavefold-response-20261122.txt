four slots compound the 3-point average (S^4), so the audible band is much darker.

=== Why ===
For a linear segment, ADAA2's two-stage divided difference reduces exactly to
  y[n] = (x[n] + x[n-1] + x[n-2]) / 3.
That 3-point average has a zero at w = 2*pi/3, i.e. f = rate/3 (14.7 kHz at
44.1 kHz, 16 kHz at 48 kHz, 32 kHz at 96 kHz), and flips phase by 180 degrees
above it. It is not a pure one-sample delay: the low-frequency group delay is
one sample, but the amplitude response is the 3-point average.

=== Limitations ===
Small-signal linearization only; large signals also fold and are not described
by this response. Chain-level, not a plugin/DAW render. No listening claim.
