# Factory content license

The 600 programs (`FS-001`–`FS-600`) and 120 MIDI phrases (`FP-001`–`FP-120`) are original data written for VOX Trance Synthesis.

No third-party preset bank, sample, or MIDI file was copied or converted. The generator is `generate_factory.py`. The installed tables are `FactoryData.inc`. Counts and categories are in `MANIFEST.txt`.

Delivered counts are the tables the plugin loads, not the capacity constants alone.
